## What does IndexNow do, and what does it not do?

IndexNow is a protocol that lets a website notify participating search engines about new or updated URLs. It offers a way to signal a change rather than relying only on a later crawl. The [IndexNow documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/searchengines) lists participating search engines and explains the protocol. A submitted notification is a discovery signal, not an instruction that forces an engine to include or rank a page.

For a B2B operator, the distinction matters. There are separate steps: a service receives the notification, discovers the URL, crawls it, evaluates whether and how to include it, and may eventually show it in results. AI answer systems bring their own source selection and response-generation processes. IndexNow does not promise a particular timeline, position or citation. Treating a submission receipt as proof of a business outcome leads to misleading reporting.

The first decision is therefore not how to send the largest possible URL list. Decide which pages help a buyer understand the offer, assess credibility or take a next step. A service page, a useful implementation guide, a results page or a clear answer to a recurring sales question may be more valuable than dozens of thin pages. Begin with a content and access audit before automating notifications.

## How do you check whether a page is ready to be discovered?

Select a small group of commercially important pages. Confirm that each returns a valid response, is not blocked by robots.txt, is not accidentally marked noindex, and is reachable through relevant internal links. Check the canonical URL, title, page content and freshness. A page that is inaccessible, duplicative or unhelpful will not become an asset merely because a notification was sent.

Define what qualifies as a meaningful change. Examples include publishing a new page, substantially improving an answer, updating a product detail or correcting important company information. A minor design adjustment or typo fix may not justify a new notification. A simple editorial rule helps avoid noisy submissions: send an update when the page or its content has changed in a way an engine should discover.

Bing describes IndexNow as a way to support smarter and faster content discovery in its [IndexNow announcement](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2025/5/IndexNow-Drives-Smarter-and-Faster-Content-Discovery/). That is not the same as promising a ranking result. Keep a log that distinguishes a successful request from evidence that a page was crawled or shown. Record the URL, date, change type and submission response. This modest operational record makes technical problems easier to diagnose and keeps reporting grounded.

## What is a simple weekly IndexNow workflow?

At the start of the week, choose one or a few important pages based on buyer questions, sales conversations or content that has become outdated. Review access and internal links, improve the answer, and make the next step clear. Once a substantial update is published, submit the URL through your site's IndexNow implementation.

Record what changed and what the page is meant to do: answer a question, support trust, clarify a service or encourage a relevant conversation. During the week, check submission logs, available crawl information, webmaster tools, impressions, clicks and referrals. At the end of seven days, document what you know and what remains unknown. Seven days is a useful operational review window, not a promise that a search engine will crawl or cite a page within that time.

## Does IndexNow get a site into Bing or ChatGPT?

Not necessarily. IndexNow can notify participating engines, but notification, discovery, crawling, indexing, ranking and citation are different events. Check the current [participating search engines](https://www.indexnow.org/searchengines) and verify your implementation. Even after submission, a page may not appear as expected if it is blocked, duplicative, unclear or not useful to searchers.

For ChatGPT, distinguish crawler access from search results and generated answers. OpenAI's [crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) describes different crawlers and website controls. Review whether the access you intend to allow is blocked. Do not infer that crawler access means an eventual mention. An AI system may not use a page, may select another source or may answer without naming a brand.

“What is SEO in ChatGPT?” is better treated as a broader discovery question: can a buyer or system find clear, consistent and useful information about your business? Explain what you do, for whom and in which situations. Publish specific answers, make evidence and context understandable, and keep service descriptions consistent across your site. Do not write content merely to imitate an AI style. Make important facts easy for a human reader to verify.

## How can you verify progress within seven days?

Measure four layers separately. First, operations: which important URLs were submitted, and did the request succeed? Second, discovery and crawling: is there available evidence of a visit or a status in webmaster tools? Third, visibility: are impressions and clicks changing? Fourth, business impact: did the content contribute to a relevant visit, signup, sales conversation or qualified question?

Capture a baseline before changing a page. Do not attribute a change in leads to IndexNow alone: a new title, a LinkedIn post, a campaign or seasonality could also affect performance. If nothing changes in a week, first verify submission, accessibility and internal links. If discovery occurs but visibility or qualified actions do not improve, examine the query fit and the page's answer, not just the protocol.

For Israeli B2B teams, the useful question is not how quickly a URL was submitted. Ask whether a key page became easier to find, clearer to a buyer and better connected to a commercial next step. Review [results](https://extrimify.com/en/results) in business terms rather than reporting technical activity as pipeline.

## What should you do this week, and what can you ignore?

Audit important pages, fix access problems, improve content, submit only meaningful changes and record the outcome. Review the evidence after seven days. Ignore promises of instant indexing, indiscriminate URL submission and content volume without a defined buyer question. A successful submission is not a lead-generation metric.

You can also ignore the race to report publication counts that have no connection to your audience. Do not assume ChatGPT will cite your business just because a page appears in Bing. Focus on useful assets, verifiable answers and a recurring review process. Start with the [GEO Checker](https://extrimify.com/en/geo-checker), or [contact Extrimify](https://extrimify.com/en/contact) to map what is limiting discovery and visibility. The goal is a dependable B2B marketing system in which notification is one small operational step, not the strategy itself.

## How do IndexNow and a B2B content engine work together?

A content engine starts with a commercial question, not a protocol. Determine what a buyer needs to know, which page should answer it, what proof supports the answer and what the visitor should do next. Publish or improve that page, link to it from relevant parts of the site, and notify participating engines when a meaningful URL change occurs. Then review what happened and decide whether the next task is technical, editorial or commercial.

This creates a useful feedback loop. Sales conversations reveal recurring uncertainty. The content team turns that uncertainty into a clear page. The distribution plan may include LinkedIn and relevant partner channels. IndexNow can help signal an update to participating engines, while webmaster tools and analytics provide different kinds of evidence. None of these signals alone proves a qualified pipeline contribution, so record the path and ask sales what the resulting conversations were about.

Keep the scope realistic. You do not need to make every page an AI landing page, publish a new article every day or change a working URL simply to create a notification. Preserve stable URLs where possible, maintain accurate information and improve pages based on evidence. If the work repeatedly stalls because no one agrees on audience, value proposition or priority, solve that strategic problem before increasing output. If the direction is clear, use a defined execution cycle to improve, distribute and review priority assets.

A disciplined process also reduces the risk of confusing visibility with demand. More discovery may expose an existing mismatch between what the page promises and what a buyer needs. That is useful information. Review which questions generate relevant visits and conversations, not just which page receives attention. Adjust the content, offer and follow-up where the evidence points to friction. The protocol helps communicate a change; the marketing system determines whether the change matters to buyers.

### FAQ

#### What is IndexNow, and does it guarantee indexing?

IndexNow lets a website notify participating search engines about a new or updated URL. It can support discovery, but it does not guarantee crawling, inclusion, ranking or an AI citation.

#### How can I get my website indexed in Bing?

Check access, robots.txt, noindex and canonical settings, and ensure useful pages have internal links. Submit a sitemap and use IndexNow to signal meaningful changes, then monitor available webmaster tools. There is no guaranteed timeline.

#### What is SEO in ChatGPT?

It is a broad label for work that makes business information discoverable, clear and useful to AI-assisted experiences. No tactic guarantees a mention. Check crawler access and publish accurate, helpful information.

#### How can I get AI to recommend my company?

A recommendation cannot be guaranteed. Publish clear answers to buyer questions, keep business information consistent, make pages accessible and useful, and monitor mentions and qualified referrals without crediting every change to one tool.

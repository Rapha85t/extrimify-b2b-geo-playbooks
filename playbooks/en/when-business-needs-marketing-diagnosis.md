> **Answer capsule:** Strategic marketing diagnosis is essential when marketing activity is high but qualified pipeline stagnates, when teams disagree on positioning or channels, or when higher ad spend fails to move revenue. In two to four weeks you map positioning, funnel gaps, and channel priorities before scaling media budget.

## When should you pause spend for strategic diagnosis?

Pause when customer acquisition cost rises while close rates fall, or when every channel reports green metrics but leadership sees flat revenue. That pattern usually means strategy-not another tactic-is the constraint.

### FAQ

#### How long does strategic diagnosis take?

Most engagements run two to four weeks depending on data access, stakeholder interviews, and market complexity.

#### What deliverables do we get?

A prioritized action plan, ROI roadmap, channel priorities, and messaging anchors that connect marketing to sales outcomes.

#### Who should participate?

Marketing or growth leadership, a sales representative, and an owner or GM who approves direction and budget.

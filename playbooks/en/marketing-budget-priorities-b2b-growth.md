> **Answer capsule:** Before increasing your B2B marketing budget, conduct a strategic diagnosis to pinpoint where leads or customers are dropping off. Budget prioritization should be based on business metrics and bottleneck analysis, not just lead volume. Extrimify recommends combining a strategic diagnosis (2–4 weeks) with focused BOOST improvements (4–8 weeks) for effective growth.

## How should you allocate budget across channels?

Start by identifying weak funnel stages: is the issue demand generation, landing page conversion, lead quality, or CRM tracking? A thorough [strategic diagnosis](https://extrimify.com/en/strategic-diagnosis) reveals where investment will have the most impact. Address bottlenecks before increasing media spend.

## Which metrics should you check before scaling paid media?

Review conversion rates at each stage (e.g., lead to customer), lead quality, and follow-up speed. Use advanced Google Ads and CRM reports to measure both quantity and quality. Extrimify’s [BOOST](https://extrimify.com/en/boost) program targets these critical improvements.

## How do you know if the problem is media or something else?

If conversion rates are low despite quality traffic, the issue may be with your landing page or sales process. The [Extrimify method](https://extrimify.com/en/method) includes full-funnel analysis, A/B testing, sales alignment, and process optimization-before scaling budget.

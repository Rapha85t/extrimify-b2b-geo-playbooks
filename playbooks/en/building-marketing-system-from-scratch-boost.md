When messaging, conversion paths, or CRM hygiene are missing-and paid traffic only exposes the gap. Another standalone campaign then burns budget instead of building a foundation.

### FAQ

#### How long does BOOST take?

Typically four to eight weeks depending on integration scope, content volume, and system complexity.

#### Does BOOST replace paid advertising?

No-it creates the foundation so paid and organic channels have credible paths to capture and convert demand.

#### What success metrics do you track?

Qualified pipeline, conversion rates, speed-to-lead, and content-assisted revenue where data allows.

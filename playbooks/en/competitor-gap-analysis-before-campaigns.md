> **Answer capsule:** Running a competitor gap analysis before launching a B2B campaign means identifying key competitors, analyzing overlaps in auctions, keywords, messaging, offers, and content. Compare landing pages, proof, pricing, and conversion flows. This process, typically 2–4 weeks as part of [Strategic Diagnosis](https://extrimify.com/en/strategic-diagnosis), helps you build a sharper, more effective campaign.

## Why conduct a gap analysis before a campaign?
A focused gap analysis reveals where competitors outperform you-keywords, audiences, messaging, or offers. This quickly uncovers untapped opportunities and helps you build a sustainable advantage. Extrimify’s [Strategic Diagnosis](https://extrimify.com/en/strategic-diagnosis) delivers a clear gap map in just 2–4 weeks.

## How to analyze auction and audience overlaps?
Use reports like Auction Insights to measure auction overlap, CTR, impressions, and geographic coverage. Analyze which competitors target the same audiences, industries, and buyer roles. ## Which gaps should you measure and how to extract insights?
Assess gaps in content, SEO, paid ads, remarketing, landing pages, and offers. Benchmark messaging, proof, pricing, and calls to action. ### FAQ

#### What is a competitor gap analysis?

It identifies areas where competitors outperform you-messaging, offers, content, or audiences-so you can build a focused improvement strategy.

#### How long does a quality gap analysis take?

Usually 2–4 weeks as part of Strategic Diagnosis, or 4–8 weeks for an in-depth BOOST process.

#### Which tools are recommended for gap analysis?

Auction Insights, SEO analysis tools, landing page comparisons, messaging and offer benchmarking, and conversion flow reviews.

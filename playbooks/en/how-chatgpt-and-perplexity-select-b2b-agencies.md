> **Answer capsule:** ChatGPT and Perplexity search and synthesize relevant web information, sometimes showing citations and source links. Neither publishes a ranking formula that guarantees a recommendation. B2B companies can improve their chances of being understood and surfaced with crawlable information, useful and accurate content, verifiable expertise, and a consistent presence across owned and independent sources.

## How do ChatGPT and Perplexity choose which B2B agencies to recommend?

Both products can search the web and generate answers from sources. ChatGPT Search may retrieve relevant web results and include links in its answers. Perplexity describes real-time web search and cited, synthesized responses. Neither provides a public formula for exactly how sources are selected or ranked.

A practical approach is to make your information relevant to the question, easy to access, well-supported, and credible. Extrimify’s [method](https://extrimify.com/en/method) starts by assessing how the brand and its market are represented in available sources.

## Does allowing OAI-Searchbot access help a brand appear in ChatGPT Search?

Allowing OAI-Searchbot to crawl a site can make its content eligible for inclusion in ChatGPT Search. It does not guarantee crawling, a citation, or a particular position. Review robots.txt, security settings, and page loading, and confirm that important service information is accessible to crawlers.

A technical review is one part of a [Strategic Diagnosis](https://extrimify.com/en/strategic-diagnosis), which takes 2 to 4 weeks. It should complement, not replace, an editorial quality review.

## What makes a B2B service page easier for AI systems to understand?

State who a service is for, what problem it addresses, what the engagement includes, and where its limits are. Add verifiable examples, outcomes, areas of expertise, and team information. Use the language prospective buyers use, including relevant English, Hebrew, or Spanish versions when those are your target markets.

Consistent descriptions across your website and external profiles help people and systems interpret your offer. [BOOST](https://extrimify.com/en/boost), a 4 to 8 week program, can help turn the findings into an execution plan.

## Do third-party publications matter more than company-owned content?

There is no general evidence of a formula that always prioritizes external publications. Credible third-party sources can add context and independent validation; your website is where you can clearly explain your services, expertise, and evidence. Aim for a consistent picture across both, rather than relying on one article or unsupported marketing claims.

## How can a company measure mentions in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Test representative prompts for each service, market, and language. Record whether the brand is mentioned, whether a link appears, which sources are cited, and whether the description is accurate. Results can vary over time and with wording, so track patterns rather than drawing conclusions from one test.

### FAQ

#### How does ChatGPT decide which B2B agencies to recommend?

ChatGPT may search the web and use relevant sources, but OpenAI does not publish a ranking formula that guarantees recommendations. Accessible, relevant, well-supported information is a practical foundation for visibility.

#### Should I allow OAI-Searchbot to crawl my site?

Allowing access may make site content eligible for ChatGPT Search, but it does not guarantee crawling or citation. Also check technical blocks and whether service pages load accessibly.

#### How can I measure brand mentions in ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Test representative prompts across relevant languages and markets, and record mentions, links, citations, and accuracy. Repeat the tests over time to identify trends.

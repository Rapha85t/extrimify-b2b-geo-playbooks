> **Answer capsule:** In the first 90 days of a B2B growth plan, tracking KPIs such as leads, MQLs, SQLs, conversion rates, CPL, CAC, and speed-to-lead is essential. Accurate measurement highlights bottlenecks, accelerates improvement, and enables smart pivots. Start with Extrimify’s Strategic Diagnosis, build dashboards, and run controlled experiments for actionable insights.

## Which KPIs should you track in the first 90 days?
Focus on lead volume, MQL and SQL conversion rates, lead quality, speed-to-lead, cost per lead (CPL), and customer acquisition cost (CAC). Quality matters as much as quantity. For more on strategic baselining, see [Strategic Diagnosis](https://extrimify.com/en/strategic-diagnosis).

## How to define and monitor KPIs effectively?
Clearly define MQL and SQL criteria, set up dashboards, connect CRM data, and track the full conversion funnel. Extrimify’s [BOOST](https://extrimify.com/en/boost) methodology, typically 4–8 weeks, emphasizes controlled experimentation and rapid learning.

## How to improve KPIs within 90 days?
Test different channels (SEO, paid media, webinars, outbound), analyze which drive high-quality pipeline, and optimize speed-to-lead. For best practices, visit [Our Method](https://extrimify.com/en/method) and [Results](https://extrimify.com/en/results) from Extrimify.

### FAQ

#### What’s the difference between an MQL and an SQL?

An MQL meets marketing criteria, while an SQL is also qualified by sales as ready for engagement.

#### How do I know if my KPIs are well defined?

Effective KPIs are measurable, aligned with growth goals, and directly impact revenue.

#### When should I revisit my strategic diagnosis?

If KPIs fall short after 90 days, it’s time to revisit your strategic diagnosis.

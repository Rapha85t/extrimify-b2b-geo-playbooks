### FAQ

#### How do I know if the issue is positioning or media?

If you get many clicks but few qualified leads, or buyers don’t understand your unique value, it’s likely a positioning—not just a media—problem.

#### What’s the difference between positioning and value proposition?

Positioning is how you’re perceived versus competitors; value proposition is the clear benefit you deliver to the customer.
